Voice Commands Help

Start listening

Tap 🎙 Speak beside the distance display and allow microphone access when prompted. In Tap to speak mode, tap the microphone for each command. Tap Stop to stop listening.

For repeated listening, open the round menu and choose Hands-free under Voice mode. The app attempts to restart listening while Play is open; your browser may still stop it. Recognition uses English (US), and browser speech support varies. If unsupported or microphone access is denied, the app displays a message.

Opening or closing this help does not start or stop listening. If the microphone is already listening, it stays on.

Throw recording

Start throw
With a disc selected, starts a throw and requests its GPS starting position. If a throw is already in progress, this marks its landing instead.
At my disc · I'm at my disc · Mark landing
Uses your current GPS position to record the active throw's landing. Start the throw and allow location access first.
In the basket
Records the final throw and opens score confirmation. Select the finishing disc first and finish or cancel any throw in progress. Confirm the score on screen.
